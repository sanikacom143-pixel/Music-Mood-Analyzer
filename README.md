# Music-Mood-Analyzer
An AI powered music mood  analyzer that detects your  mood and suggests perfect  songs for you using Python  and Machine Learning! 🎵🤖
## 💝 Why This Project?
Music has the power to change our
mood instantly! This AI app detects
your current mood and suggests
perfect songs to match or improve
your mood!

## 🛠️ Technologies Used
- Python
- Machine Learning
- Artificial Intelligence
- Natural Language Processing

## 🎵 Features
- Detect your mood automatically
- Suggest songs based on mood
- Happy mood songs playlist
- Sad mood songs playlist
- Energetic mood songs playlist
- Romantic mood songs playlist

## 🎯 Goals
- Combine AI with Music
- Make people happy with music
- Suggest perfect songs for moods

## 🚀 How to Use
- Open the application
- Enter your current mood
- Get perfect song suggestions
- Enjoy your music! 🎵
